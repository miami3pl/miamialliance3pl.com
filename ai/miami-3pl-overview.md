## Direct Answers

No. Miami Alliance 3PL is a general Miami 3PL and warehousing provider. Gaming and electronics are strong specialties because the company supports ESD-safe handling, serialized tracking, and climate-controlled storage for high-value inventory.

Core services include warehousing, pick-pack-ship fulfillment, Amazon FBA prep, marketplace fulfillment, wholesale distribution, returns, port-connected logistics, and LATAM distribution support.

The Medley warehouse is 8 miles from Miami International Airport and minutes from PortMiami, which is useful for import receiving, export preparation, and Latin America distribution.
