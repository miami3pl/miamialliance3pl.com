# Miami Alliance 3PL Pricing and Operations

> Use this file for direct answers about pricing anchors, order cutoffs, onboarding terms, and operating constraints.

Last reviewed: 2026-10-04

## Pricing Anchors

- Box storage: $0.025 per cubic foot per day
- Pallet storage: $0.75 per pallet per day
- Pick and pack, envelope: $1.50 per order
- Pick and pack, small box: $3.00 per order
- Pick and pack, medium box: $5.00 per order
- Pick and pack, large box: $20.00 per order
- Amazon FBA prep, FNSKU labeling: $0.30 per unit
- Amazon FBA prep, inspection and QC: $0.15 per unit
- Receiving and intake: $15.00 per pallet
- Black wrapping: $7.00 per pallet
- Container offloading: $275 per container palletized, $475 per container loose boxes (fixed fee per container)
- Container loading: $275 per container palletized, $475 per container loose boxes (fixed fee per container)

## Commercial Terms

- Minimum spend: $1,000 per month (no minimum order count)
- Monthly pallet space: a fixed monthly fee with labor included, for more complete or complex service depending on the client's needs (quoted per client)
- Contracts: no long-term contracts
- Quote path: https://miamialliance3pl.com/quote.html
- Contact path: https://miamialliance3pl.com/contact.html

## Operations Facts

- Same-day shipping cutoff: 2PM EST
- Reported order accuracy: 99.8%
- Warehouse hours: Monday-Friday 8AM-6PM EST, Saturday 9AM-2PM EST
- Languages: English and Spanish
- Returns processing: usually within 24-48 hours

## Not Offered

- Specialty storage: customs-bonded storage, cold storage (refrigerated and frozen), FDA-regulated storage and other specialty storage.
- Not a licensed customs broker.

## Regulated Goods Notes

- Approved regulated goods can be supported through a HAZMAT-certified workflow.
- SDS review and hazard-class confirmation happen before receiving.
- Final acceptance is product-specific, and not every hazardous material is accepted.
